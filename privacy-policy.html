Privacy Policy for Narra

Last updated: 4 June 2026

Narra ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an offline, on-device bilingual e-reader and reading application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. The short version: Narra does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. No Data Collection

Narra is designed to be 100% offline-first. The App does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or device data. We do not operate any backend service that receives your information, and we do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. On-Device Processing Only

All core features — including reading, AI-assisted translation, glossary lookups, and grammar tips — run entirely on your device. Your books, reading progress, settings, and any text you read or translate never leave your device and are never sent to us or to any third party.

3. Files and Storage

Books you import and any data the App generates (such as translations, reading position, and preferences) are stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. This data remains under your control. Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device.

4. AI Model Downloads

To enable on-device translation, the App may download an AI language model file after installation. This download retrieves model files only; it does not transmit any of your personal data, reading content, or usage information. Once downloaded, the model runs entirely offline on your device.

5. Permissions

The App may request access to local storage solely to let you import and read your own book files. Any such access is used only on your device for the App's reading functionality and is never transmitted off the device.

6. No Data Sharing

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to third parties. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other party.

7. Children's Privacy

Narra is intended for a general audience. The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, the App poses no data-collection risk to users of any age.

8. Security

Since your data stays on your device, its security is governed by your device's own protections (for example, your screen lock and operating-system security). We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
